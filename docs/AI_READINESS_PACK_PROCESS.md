# AI Readiness Pack Process

## Customer path

1. The customer buys the $495 pack from the Secure Business AI product page.
2. The payment provider redirects them to `/ai-readiness-intake` after successful payment.
3. The intake form emails the customer confirmation and emails the delivery brief to `info@securebusinessai.com.au`.
4. Prepare the editable draft, send it to the customer for review, then finalise the pack.

## Delivery checklist

1. Check the supplied website and public business information.
2. Draft `llms.txt` with the correct public URLs.
3. Draft business facts page copy: services, locations, trust details, FAQs, and contact pathways.
4. Provide appropriate schema suggestions or snippets for the website platform.
5. Provide suggested `robots.txt` additions without replacing existing rules.
6. Write platform-appropriate install notes.
7. Send the editable draft for customer approval.
8. Deliver the approved files and instructions by email.

## Important safeguards

- Do not ask for or accept passwords, website logins, or payment details through the intake form or email.
- Treat all claims as customer-provided until confirmed on the public website.
- Do not promise rankings, citations, or particular AI search outcomes.
- Keep the customer-facing wording plain-English and do not disclose internal research tools.
