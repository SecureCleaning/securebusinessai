# White-Label Chat + Voice Refresh

Updated: 2026-05-13

## Practical Recommendation

Use a two-lane supplier strategy:

1. **GoHighLevel first for launch operations**
   - Best fit for CRM, webchat, Conversation AI, Voice AI, automations, billing, client sub-accounts, and white-labelled SaaS delivery in one system.
   - Best when the priority is speed, fewer tools, and enough capability to start selling.

2. **AiDial as the preferred Australian voice partner to qualify**
   - Best fit when Australian hosting, Australian voices, compliance, call recordings, transcripts, handover, and local partner support matter.
   - Stronger fit for healthcare, legal, finance, government, and compliance-conscious service businesses.

## Why This Changed

The older research already recommended GoHighLevel as the launch default and AiDial as the AU outreach target. The refreshed vendor material makes AiDial more compelling because it now presents a clear agency partner offer with partner discounting, multi-client management, Australian infrastructure, compliance controls, and phone/chat/SMS positioning.

## Offer Positioning

Use plain customer language:

**AI Website Chatbot**
An assistant on the website that answers common customer questions, guides visitors to the right service, and collects the details needed for your team to follow up.

**AI Voice Receptionist**
A phone assistant that answers calls, collects the caller's details, books or routes enquiries, and sends your team a clear summary so fewer opportunities are missed.

**Connected AI Front Desk**
The preferred package: website chat + phone answering + SMS/email handover trained from the same business information where the chosen supplier supports it.

## Due Diligence Questions

Ask each provider:

1. Can we fully white-label the customer-facing dashboard, emails, notifications, and domain?
2. Can voice and chat use the same client knowledge base or training source?
3. Can each client be trained from their website, documents, FAQs, service areas, pricing rules, and booking process?
4. Where is data hosted and processed, especially for Australian clients?
5. Are call recordings, transcripts, summaries, analytics, and human handover included?
6. Can we set our own client pricing and keep the margin?
7. What is the real setup time for a trades client, medical client, and professional services client?
8. What happens if the AI gets stuck, asks the wrong question, or needs to hand over to a person?

## Decision

For the website right now, show the chatbot as a Secure Business AI service and lead-capture demo. The live production widget should remain provider-neutral until the final supplier is chosen. Once the supplier is selected, replace the demo script with that provider's embed or API-backed assistant.
